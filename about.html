Why I Made This Website

This website is about Halloween costume ideas. Its purpose is to help people find inspiration when deciding what to wear for Halloween.

Some people like scary costumes, while others prefer funny or simple ones. I wanted to include different ideas so visitors can find something they like.

About Halloween

Halloween is celebrated on October 31. People celebrate by wearing costumes, decorating their homes, attending parties, and going trick-or-treating.

One of the best parts of Halloween is being able to express yourself through a costume.

My Goals for This Website

  • Share different costume ideas.
  • Help people find affordable costumes.
  • Make it easy to browse Halloween inspiration.
  • Encourage people to be creative.

For more information about Halloween, visit History.com's Halloween page.
